# Support

- **Feature requests:** [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/MasteryHub-ITS/plane-vscode-extension/issues/new?template=feature_request.yml)

- **Docs:** [README.md](./README.md), [FEATURES.md](./FEATURES.md), [CONTRIBUTING.md](./CONTRIBUTING.md)
